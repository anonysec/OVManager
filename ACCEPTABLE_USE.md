# Acceptable use guidance

OVManager is management software. The server operator, not this project, operates the VPN service and is responsible for users, traffic, abuse reports, hosting-provider terms, and applicable law.

Operators should prohibit and respond to abuse including spam, unauthorized scanning or access, malware, credential attacks, unlawful content distribution, and infringement. Use quotas, expiry, audit events, and account disabling as operational tools, but do not treat them as guarantees of compliance.

Operators should maintain their own end-user terms, privacy notice, abuse contact, incident process, and lawful-request procedure appropriate to their jurisdiction and service.

This guidance does not add a field-of-use restriction to the MIT software license. The license grant remains in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
